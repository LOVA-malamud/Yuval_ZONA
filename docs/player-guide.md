# Player guide

Destroy the enemy King while protecting yours. Azure has your commander and an AI ally; Ember has two AI commanders. Gold, wood, upgrades and recruited troops belong to the team. Fallen commanders respawn; King defeat ends the match.

## Lead a local push

Choose a route with **1/2/3** or the route selector, then recruit. Existing troops keep their route. Stay near a group and press **Q / Rally**: allied army troops within 300 world units receive +15% movement speed and +20% attack rate for eight seconds. It excludes commanders, workers and structures, does not stack, and has a 30-second cooldown.

Press **R / Regroup** to gather allied troops within 400 around safe positions near your activation point for eight seconds. Combat takes priority; afterward troops resume their original lanes. Repeating Regroup replaces the temporary destination. These commands affect nearby troops rather than the whole army.

Click a visible enemy to focus attacks, then hold Space to strike. Invalid targets, targets outside detection range and targets behind terrain clear automatically; ordinary nearby targeting continues. On touch devices, drag on the left to move; auto-attack works independently of movement. Tap enemies to focus and use the on-screen commands. Release an ability button inside it to activate along your last movement facing; release outside to cancel. Hold and drag the minimap to scout; release to follow your commander again. Purchasing uses collapsible drawers.

## Coordinate your ally

**Orders** offers Auto, Push North/Center/South, Defend and Escort. Orders persist through the ally’s death and respawn. Immediate threats and low health take priority. Escort returns to base while your commander is dead. Explicit orders cancel conflicting construction plans and reservations.

The panel shows current activity, planned purchase, reserved resources and recent shared spending. AI commanders respect construction budgets; you can spend freely. Spending or changes to a build pad invalidate plans that can no longer succeed.

## Read the battle

The minimap marks King danger, lost towers, threatened workers and forming pushes. Nearby repeated alerts are suppressed for ten seconds. Timber is finite: shallow home stock gives way to larger exposed groves; neutral tower pads are construction opportunities. There is no capture currency.

Upgrade cards show current and next benefit, cost and maximum level. The final recap records duration, each commander’s shared spending, recruit purchases, King damage and rallies, plus worker losses and tower turnover. Recruits credit their purchasing commander; worker deposits belong to the team.

## Learn and restart

Pause offers Tutorial, a fresh match and a difficulty selector. Tutorial is a separate playable match covering movement, attacks, routes and recruitment, ally orders and Rally, a tower and the King objective. Restart repeats it; Skip starts an ordinary match.

Easy reacts more slowly and avoids coordinated rallies; Standard uses ordinary tactical behavior; Hard reacts faster and coordinates pressure and rallies. Starting resources and unit stats remain equal. Difficulty applies to the next match. English and Russian, audio, display and guidance preferences persist.

Focus loss pauses play and clears held input. Escape or system Back closes the current reference/settings panel before resuming a paused match. Physical phone comfort still requires device playtesting.

## Combat and recovery

Use **Shift** to dash, **F** to guard and **C** for a heavy strike, along your last movement facing. Guard reduces frontal damage; a heavy strike commits your facing during its wind-up and briefly stuns an unguarded commander or troop. Dash has no invulnerability. Movement, damage and offensive actions interrupt base recovery: use Interact within 180 units of your King to channel 60 HP/second, with a 30-second cooldown after a valid start.

Build Guard, Splash or Long-range towers at pads. Splash hits nearby enemies; Long-range towers have a close-range dead zone. Secure exposed timber as home stock runs out.
